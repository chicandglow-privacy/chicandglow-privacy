Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

ChicandGlow.edit respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you interact with our Pinterest application.

1. About the application

ChicandGlow.edit is a content management application used to create and manage organic Pinterest content for the ChicandGlow brand, including lifestyle, beauty, fashion, home decor and product discovery content.

2. Information accessed

When you authorize our application through Pinterest, the application may access information permitted by the Pinterest API and the permissions granted by you.

This may include information relating to your Pinterest account, boards and Pins that are necessary to create, manage and publish content on your behalf.

We do not request or collect your Pinterest password.

3. How information is used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to create and manage Pins and boards, publish organic Pinterest content, review relevant account information, and monitor content performance.

4. Third-party services

Our application may interact with Pinterest and other services necessary to provide content management and publishing functions. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information accessed through our application and limit access to information to what is necessary for the intended purpose.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the intended service or comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Children's privacy

Our application is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
